# Synthetic demo materials

These six files are fictional and contain no real personal data. Upload them to a disposable FileMind account to demonstrate Chinese text extraction, keyword search, citations, and job-application pack suggestions. They are Markdown/TXT so the demo does not depend on external PDFs or OCR model downloads. The sample job posting and project retrospective can be compared with the resume and certificate checklist.

Suggested searches after processing:

- `图神经网络`
- `Python 数据分析 实习`
- `项目成果 指标`
- `窗口函数`
- `岗位需求 证书 待补充`

Suggested library layout: put the resume, certificate checklist, and job posting in `求职资料`; put the course notes, research card, and project retrospective in `课程与研究`. Mark the resume and certificate checklist as favorites. A manual export of those two files demonstrates the downloadable application pack without an AI key.

Example questions when DeepSeek is configured:

- Which course notes discuss graph neural networks? Cite the relevant passages.
- Which resume claims are supported by the uploaded materials, and what evidence is missing?

Do not enable AI for this account unless a DeepSeek key is configured and you intend to send selected excerpts to that provider.
